# Kingdom Wars Game Rules

Kingdom Wars is a strategic multiplayer game where each tower fights for itself.

## Starting Conditions

Every player starts with:

- 100 Health Points
- 20 Coins

## Objective

Survive, negotiate, and defeat all opponents.

## Resources and Economy

After each Negotiation Phase, you receive 20 coins. Income depends on your tower level.

You can spend coins on:

- Troops: 1 coin = 1 soldier
- Armor: 1 coin = 1 armor, which destroys 1 enemy soldier
- Tower upgrade (Level 1): 50 coins
- Tower upgrade (Level 2): 88 coins

## Turn Overview

- A game lasts up to 25 turns.
- Each turn has two phases: Negotiation and Combat.

You can follow game progress in the upper-right corner of the screen, including:

- Current phase
- Turn timer
- Turn number
- Fatigue information

## Negotiation Phase

Duration: 20 seconds.

During the Negotiation Phase, you can communicate with other players:

- Offer friendship
- Send a threat of attack by clicking the flag that matches the opponent's tower

Notes:

- You can send only one negotiation action per opponent.
- Messages sent during this phase become visible in the next phase.
- Use the Combat Log to identify allies and potential attackers.

## Combat Phase

Duration: 20 seconds.

During Combat, you allocate coins and troops for attack and defense:

- Choose how many troops to send to each opponent
- Choose how many coins to spend on defense
- Upgrade your tower to increase future income

You can always see your planned spending and remaining coins.
You may adjust coin allocation until the Combat Phase ends.

## Diplomacy Log

Review sent and received messages to track alliances.
Monitoring private conversations helps you identify threats and plan your next move.

## Duel Mode

When only two players remain, the game enters Duel Mode:

- Negotiations stop
- Only direct combat remains

Duel Mode does not apply to games with AI players.

## Fatigue System

Fatigue starts:

- After 25 combats, or
- After the 5th turn when only 2 players remain

Fatigue damage progression:

| Turn After Fatigue Starts | Damage |
| --- | --- |
| 1st | 10 |
| 2nd | 20 |
| 3rd | 40 |
| 4th | 80 |

This system speeds up the game, increases damage each turn, and prevents endless standoffs.
Fatigue damage is shown in the top-right corner.

## Results

At the end of the game, the Results modal appears:

- Winner: the player whose tower did not fall
- Game statistics: tower level, resources, armor, and attacks
- Replay: watch the full game to review decisions, learn from mistakes, and study strategies

## Final Note

The strongest strategy wins.
